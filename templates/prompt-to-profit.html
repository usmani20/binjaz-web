AI Should Amplify Human Excellence, Not Replace It

Most people use AI to write emails or draft presentations. That only scratches the surface. The real advantage belongs to those who can look at a business, spot where intelligent agents create value, and build them.
Prompt to Profit develops that capability: the critical thinking, problem solving and business judgement to turn AI into real results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Agents are built on Promptev, so no coding is needed. If you can think through a business problem clearly, you can build an agent to solve it.

Is this program only for students?

Not at all. The cohort brings together working professionals, freelancers, graduates and school & college students. Everyone learns side by side, which makes the discussions richer.

What do I need to attend?

A laptop or desktop with a stable internet connection. All sessions run live on Google Meet.

How does the group rate work?

When 2 or more people register together, each pays PKR 15,000. Each person fills in their own registration form and mentions the names of the others in their group.

Can I register my child?

Yes. Choose "My child / children" on the registration form and you can add up to three children in a single submission. Siblings qualify for the group rate.

What happens after I register?

Our team will contact you on WhatsApp within 24 hours to confirm your enrollment and share payment details.
